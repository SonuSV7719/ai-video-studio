## 🎬 Scene-by-Scene Shot List & Breakdown

### Scene 1: The Acoustic Hook & Lock-On (0.0s – 4.5s)
* **Audio Track**: Soft acoustic guitar fingerpicking starts; vocal lead enters.
* **Lyrics**: *"Dekho dekho..."*
* **Visual Composition**:
  - Background dims into deep obsidian darkness (`spotlight` effect).
  - Silhouette of the singer/character illuminated by a single warm golden top-light.
  - Cyan face-tracking corner brackets (`locked-on`) smoothly acquire the subject's face at `1.35x` zoom.
* **Text / Overlays**:
  - Word pill floats under chin: `[ DEKHO DEKHO ]` with pulsing amber glow.
* **Audio Cue**: Subtle UI lock chime synced to the first vocal entry at `1.2s`.

---

### Scene 2: Behind-The-Head Typographic Drop (4.5s – 9.0s)
* **Audio Track**: Acoustic rhythm picks up with rhythmic palm-mute thumps.
* **Lyrics**: *"Kaisi baatein yahan ki..."*
* **Visual Composition**:
  - Giant typographic word **`"BAATEIN"`** (Archivo Black, 220px, 25% opacity) drops from the ceiling into the wall *behind* the singer's head (`backdrop-word` effect).
  - The singer stays in crisp foreground using MediaPipe selfie segmentation.
* **Text / Overlays**:
  - Kinetic subtitle card with staggered word highlight: `kaisi` -> `baatein` -> `yahan` -> `ki`.
* **Motion / Camera**: Subtle drift left with 3D parallax between background text and foreground singer.

---

### Scene 3: The Paradox Split & Crayon Mark (9.0s – 14.0s)
* **Audio Track**: Vocal shifts into melancholic yearning.
* **Lyrics**: *"Hai saath par hain saath na bhi..."*
* **Visual Composition**:
  - Emotional tension visualization: Frame splits momentarily with an intimate warm B-roll silhouette on top and the singer on bottom (`broll-split`).
  - Red hand-drawn crayon circle and arrow (`crayon-write`) sketch themselves around the singer's eye line.
* **Text / Overlays**:
  - Frosted glass lyric pill centered with pulsing blur reflection (`--bg-card-glass`).
* **Motion / Camera**: Fast 2-frame punch-in on *"saath na bhi"* at `11.8s`.

---

### Scene 4: Floating Emotional Waveform & Liquid Glass (14.0s – 19.5s)
* **Audio Track**: Melodic crescendo leading toward the chorus.
* **Lyrics**: *"Kya itni aasaan hai yeh be-rukhi..."*
* **Visual Composition**:
  - Translucent liquid glass card (`liquid-glass-graph` / `glass-widgets`) floats under the singer's chin.
  - An animated frequency waveform climbs across the card in rhythm with the vocal vibrato.
* **Text / Overlays**:
  - Poetic line typewriter reveal in `Instrument Serif`: *"be-rukhi"*.
* **Motion / Camera**: Smooth slow push-in (`slow-push`), background room softly melting into bokeh.

---

### Scene 5: The Chorus Climax — HUSN Explosion (19.5s – 25.0s)
* **Audio Track**: Chorus drop! Vocal belt: *"Husn tera tauba tauba..."*
* **Lyrics**: *"Husn tera tauba tauba..."*
* **Visual Composition**:
  - **The Showstopper Moment**: Full lights cut, electric coral-red back-glow strikes.
  - Giant glowing text **`"HUSN"`** (300px, `#FF4A3D`) slams directly behind the singer's silhouette (`backdrop-word` + `spotlight`).
  - Glowing ember dust particles float upward (`dot-reveal`).
* **Text / Overlays**:
  - Kinetic lyric cards: `[ HUSN ]` ➔ `[ TAUBA TAUBA ]` with yellow active highlighting.
* **Motion / Camera**: Dynamic camera punch-in (`lean-in`, 1.45x) hitting precisely on the word *"Husn"*.

---

### Scene 6: Outro Loop & Infinite Orbit (25.0s – 30.0s)
* **Audio Track**: Acoustic guitar resolves on a resonant open chord, ready to loop back to 0.0s.
* **Lyrics**: *"Husn tera tauba... [Guitar Sustains]"*
* **Visual Composition**:
  - Polaroids and vinyl lyric cards circle 3D around the singer's head (`orbit` effect: passing in front at the bottom, behind at the top).
  - Floating Follow pill (`tap` effect) flips into "Listening" at `28.2s`.
* **Motion / Camera**: Camera eases back to wide shot, seamlessly matching Scene 1's starting composition for infinite loop replay on Instagram/Shorts.
